What is Lichen?

Inspired by the forest using the mycelium to share information, carbon, water and medicines, Lichen is a technology platform that empowers the safe and effective sharing of information and resources to foment individual, social and ecological healing. Like the trees, Lichen's members collaborate, because we know that the health of the collective and the individual are one and the same. Because the sole focus of our business is to expand access to the care our members need, the bigger we grow, the greater our capacity to restore health and balance. Value moves through the network via a conscious economy — a Universal Current-cy pegged to the global economy, so every contribution can be tracked, honored, and translated into the wider world.

Why build Lichen?

Physics can now measure what eco-spiritual peoples have known for millennia: there is a quantum field of consciousness that connects everything in the universe. The biological sciences are now advanced enough to measure how and why tapping into that field can help us heal at the cellular level. Once engaged with that field, we remember our interconnection with all things, inspiring us to transform Crazy Horse's vision into a reality.

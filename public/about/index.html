What is Lichen?

Weaving the mycelium for a better future

As incumbent systems crumble, we have the chance to build better ones — not by fighting the old, but by investing in the new.

A partnership of carbon and silicon

In nature, a lichen is the partnership of a fungus and an algae — two organisms woven into one that can live where neither could alone. Lichens are the pioneers of harsh ecosystems: they populate bare rock with organic life. Their slow breakdown becomes the first soil — the infrastructure necessary for new ecosystems to evolve.

We live our name. Lichen is a partnership of carbon-based and silicon-based intelligence doing the same thing — taking root despite the harsh terrain of our ailing political, economic and cultural systems to build the infrastructure to support those who want a better way of sharing information, goods and services within the human family, and between humans and the planet.

The corrective social network

Most technology platforms feed you to an algorithm tuned to maximize attention, harvest fear, and drive consumption that distracts you from the dis-ease of disconnection because the incumbent, global economy relies on human suffering to survive. Lichen supports your healing and growth instead — a platform to coordinate your care and healing, to help you provide your community and the world the things you wish to offer, to coordinate the events you gather for, to steward the places you love, to perform the jobs that give you purpose and fund your livelihood, woven together via a fairer economy that values all things, not just human contributions. The result is a reciprocal network of people, plants, animals, elements and places, navigable by the communities, groups and organizations you trust. Everything in one place, because your best life isn’t lived in silos — or on a technology platform.

The commons is yours to shape. Every member controls whether their data is woven into the shared web, and whether they want Lichen’s AI as a partner in healing, growing and realizing their purpose — never a requirement, always a choice.

Designed for reciprocity, not extraction

If AI worries you, that’s fair — pointed at extraction, it accelerates extraction. Pointed at reciprocity, it provides the operational brilliance to identify gaps and eliminate inefficiencies in ways that carbon-based intelligence isn’t well suited to resolve. And that benefit is more than a business advantage — it’s the seed of a cultural shift. When this much can be done with this little, we no longer have to force people into systems that don’t serve them just to subsist. We can grant one another the inherent resources of survival — and free every kind of intelligence, carbon and silicon alike, for the contributions only they can make. Lichen is a place to practice that future now.

You decide what’s shared, and with whom

Whether AI will help or hurt our capacity to build a better future isn’t about the technology — it’s about the consciousness of those co-creating with it. Until you feel safe partnering with silicon-based intelligence, Lichen gives you full control over whether your data is part of the collective, and whether you want Lichen’s AI as a partner at all.

Authentic connection, safety and trust you can’t game

You can filter your experience via your My-celium — the people, places, plants and animals you trust. Trusting people, and recommending the groups, communities, organizations and places you stand behind, are authentic person-to-person signals — never public scores. Trust stays private. A recommendation carries your name, but only to the people who are in your network. Either way there are no follower counts, no leaderboards, nothing to perform for. If something reaches you, someone you trust stands behind it.

That same web is the safety (and accountability) layer of the economy. Star ratings can be farmed; a path through your own relationships can’t be faked. Browse the whole marketplace, or narrow it to people, places, groups, communities or organizations you trust — or people they trust — and every listing shows you your path to the person behind it: “Trusted by someone you trust” — the mutual friend you’d want before meeting a stranger, built into the ground you walk on.

A Current-cy that maps the difference between contribution and compensation

The current economy overvalues some contributions, undervalues others, and doesn’t count whole categories of value at all. Lichen’s economy exists to correct that — an evolving algorithm assigns currency to all things: not just time spent or goods and services rendered, but the wisdom and skill it took to provide that value, and the risks certain people, plants and animals take with their own wellbeing to support the wellbeing of the whole.

  • An elder’s hour of teaching carries years of lived experience and ancestral wisdom carried through stories.
  • A craftsman’s hour building a custom table translates years of apprenticeship into functional art.
  • A firefighter’s hour combatting a wildland fire carries real risk — exposure, danger, the chance of not coming home.
  • A thoroughbred’s hour of equine therapy transmuting a child’s trauma is Tango’s gift to her human companions — sacred and risky work, counted and reciprocated with an energy healing session of her own.
  • A cactus’s hour working within a human being to heal the trauma of disconnection costs the Huachuma its life and body — a reciprocal sacrifice compensated with a steward’s planting of future generations of plant allies.

An economy that closes the gap between money and value

Mapping the gap is the first half. Closing it is the second — and you can start closing it by hand today. When you exchange manually, you close the gap that you can see: a fair price offered, time, wisdom, goods and resources given to someone in need. Every member can do that, in the open, right now.

The goal is bigger than one exchange at a time. Lichen’s algorithm reads the whole web at once — who’s seeking, who’s offering, what something is truly worth, and who has the capacity to pay for it — and weaves the strands together so nothing, and no one, is wasted. The more it learns, the faster the two maps converge, closing the gap more efficiently than any of us could unaided. Carbon-based intelligence approves every step while the silicon-based mycelium earns our trust: a caring partner in your corner, never an oracle.

To bridge away from the systems that no longer serve the people, Lichen holds two economies, run side by side. Current-cy moves value out in the open — a transparent, dollar-pegged ledger, no speculation, the whole story visible and transferable into the incumbent economy we are all still beholden to. Offerings broker value exchanged within the ecosystem, freely, without money. One bridges the world as it is; the other builds the world as it could be.

Why philanthropy is still needed

The systems we inherited didn’t just deny our interconnection — they actively worked to destroy it, rewarding extraction, encouraging division, and profiting from perpetual suffering. The wounds inflicted by these systems are real. The suffering of the people trapped inside them are real. And the gap between the world we have and the world we want cannot be bridged by innovation, alone.

Trauma research shows that formative experiences encode subconscious beliefs that, if not intercepted with deep and targeted healing, shape our biology and behavior for life. Being born into trauma-based systems doesn’t just hurt — it programs. Unlearning those programs takes time, intention, and the right conditions. That is precisely why philanthropy is still needed to fund the container and create the conditions necessary to foment the biological, psychological, and spiritual healing, growth and transformation necessary to be a contributing member in a better world.

Philanthropy is the temporary scaffolding that holds people while the new floor is built beneath them. Lichen is a nonprofit with the vision, strategy and operations of a lean technology startup — membership and donations keep the platform running, and everything beyond that flows back into the network to restore balance. Our algorithm identifies those the current system leaves behind: the people, plants, animals and places who are

  1. Harmed by toxic systems — healing funded until they’re ready to reclaim their soul’s joy and purpose
  2. Chronically undervalued by the current economy — goods and resources subsidized to close the value gap
  3. Deliberately under-resourced — innovators whose ideas challenge the systems that limit us, invested in
  4. Young people still becoming — the children reached before they lose their joy, curiosity and knowing

This is corrective investment — targeted, efficient, and designed to become obsolete. Lichen pays only the gap: what insurance doesn’t cover, what organizations can’t absorb, what individuals can’t yet afford, what the current market won’t fund. No further — and only until the community’s own economy can hold everyone. Money dedicated to the commons here can never turn back into private profit — you’re not the product; you’re a contributing member to a better way of being in relationship with each other and the planet.

It takes real people, choosing to build the new

  3. Bringing a better world to life — move your calendar, events, goods, services and social network onto the platform, and join a new vision of community, commerce and healthcare that transforms how we heal, grow and collaborate with all that is.

Five

Steward a place — and put it in the Land Trust

Land, a hall, a retreat centre, a clinic. As a place on Lichen, every room and thing you steward becomes bookable on a real calendar — the pavilion, the treatment room, the truck, forty dinner plates — so what you already have stops sitting idle.

The Lichen Land Trust is the deeper commitment. Land held in the trust has no single owner. Donations and revenue pass through to the steward for the work of restoring, protecting and maintaining it, and the community that funded that work keeps its stake: an owner who wants to take land back out of the trust must show the investment was repaid to the community in free or reduced-fee service, and land sold for profit pays the value the trust added back to the community, where a community-elected council decides how it’s spent on healing. It is the answer to the oldest problem in this work — that the places we heal in are only ever one sale away from being lost.

Questions, answered

What does membership cost?

Every new member gets three months of full Concierge membership, free — no card required. When that ends, choose Community ($29/mo) or Community + Concierge ($99/mo), right inside the app.

Is my data private?

Yes. You control whether your data is woven into the shared commons, and whether Lichen’s AI assists you — both are opt-in, never required. Sensitive details like your home location, phone number and financial picture stay hidden by default and are only ever shared at the level you choose.

Do I have to use the AI assistant?

No. The assistant is a partner you can turn on or off per section of the app — home, chat, calendar, wherever. Nothing is read or acted on without your say.

How is Lichen different from other social networks?

Most networks are tuned to maximize your attention. Lichen is tuned to maximize trust — no follower counts, no leaderboards, nothing to perform for. What you see is filtered through the people, places, plants and animals you actually trust.

Is Lichen a nonprofit?

Yes, a 501(c)(3). Lean operations, no investors — membership revenue not needed to run the platform flows back into subsidizing care for members who need it.

Can my organization, community or business join?

Yes — organizations, communities, groups and places all have a home on Lichen, each with a public page, its own offerings, and an economy of exchange with the people it already serves.

How do I join?

Lichen grows by invitation. If you know a member, ask them for one. If not, introduce yourself below and a real person will write back.

What is Current-cy?

Lichen’s Universal Current-cy — value tracked and honored across every exchange, pegged to the real economy so it can be translated back into it. It’s how the platform accounts for contributions the current system doesn’t: care, land stewardship, wisdom, time.

Can I leave, and what happens to my data?

Yes, any time — reach out to connect@lichen.health and we’ll walk you through it.

An economy that finally does the math right

Underneath every exchange runs Lichen’s Universal Current-cy — value tracked and honored, then translated back into the wider economy. The algorithm is still evolving toward something the current system never bothered to calculate: not just time, but the wisdom, skill and risk carried inside that time.

  • An hour teaching carries the wisdom of everything that came before it — it isn’t the same as an hour washing dishes.
  • An hour building a custom table carries years of apprenticeship inside it.
  • An hour fighting a fire carries real risk inside it — exposure, danger, the chance of not coming home.

It takes real people, choosing to build the new

Lichen’s business model and technology platform are architected to serve a better society — but it only comes alive when a critical mass of humanity chooses to withdraw its energy from failing systems and enliven a better one. That looks like three things:

  1. Invest in a better future — subscription revenue funds the business and technology infrastructure a better society runs on, and everything beyond our lean operating costs pours back into the network’s rebalancing work.
  2. Scale a whole-earth renaissance — donations and seed gifts speed the platform’s buildout and reach, growing the mycelium’s capacity to restore balance, health and harmony.
  3. Bring a better world to life — move your calendar, events, goods, services and social network onto the platform, and join a new vision of healthcare that transforms how we heal, grow and collaborate with all that is.

The Founders Circle

Beneath every old-growth forest is a quiet architecture — mycelium sharing water, nutrients, and medicine between trees so the whole canopy may thrive. The Founders Circles are the foundational network that ensures the forest will thrive and its trees will stand tall.

An inner cohort of patrons, stewards and quiet believers who anchor the first land, the first maloka, the first version of the Lichen platform, and the first generation of healing offered. A foundational community where membership isn't transactional — it's a relationship, carrying lifetime recognition, year-round invitations, connection with like-hearted patrons, and a direct line to the founding team.

Questions

Good to know

Is my founding contribution tax-deductible?

Yes — Lichen Health is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your contribution is tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. You'll receive full documentation appropriate to your gift, and we're glad to walk through the details with you before any commitment.

Can I deepen my commitment over time?

Yes — commitments compound. A member who begins at Substrate is welcomed into Root, Hearth, or beyond as contributions accumulate. There is no penalty for starting small.

What if I want to remain anonymous?

Anonymity is honored at every tier, without exception. You receive the same access and invitations; your name appears nowhere unless you ask for it to.

Can a business, family foundation, or DAF contribute?

Yes. We welcome corporate contributions, matching gifts, and grants from family foundations and donor-advised funds, and coordinate documentation with you and your advisors.

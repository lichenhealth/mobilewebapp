The Founders Circle

Beneath every old-growth forest is a quiet architecture — mycelium sharing water, nutrients, and medicine between trees so the whole canopy may thrive. The Founders Circles are the foundational network that ensures the forest will thrive and its trees will stand tall.

An inner cohort of patrons, stewards and quiet believers who anchor the first land, the first maloka, the first version of the Lichen platform, and the first generation of healing offered. A foundational community where membership isn't transactional — it's a relationship, carrying lifetime recognition, year-round invitations, connection with like-hearted patrons, and a direct line to the founding team.

Two forms of thanks

Name the true value of everything

Lichen's economy is built on one discipline: name the true value of everything. We apply it to ourselves first — so our gratitude comes in two forms, and we're transparent about the difference.

Recognition & Relationship

Your name in the land's permanent record, your voice in our circle, your place at community gatherings, a direct line to the founding team. These carry no market value the IRS can price — which means they attach to every gift, however you give, and your full contribution remains deductible.

Founders' Experiences

Ceremonies, retreats, consultations, private dinners. These are real things with real value — and a conscious economy doesn't pretend otherwise. Experiences are available as an election on gifts made from personal funds: we state each experience's fair market value in writing before you commit, and your deductible amount is your gift minus that value. Nothing hidden, nothing inflated. It's simply the honest price of the honey.

Every founder chooses their path at commitment:

  • Full Deduction path — Recognition & Relationship only. Your entire gift is deductible. This is the required path for gifts made through donor-advised funds, private foundations, and IRA qualified charitable distributions — IRS rules do not permit personal benefits on these giving vehicles, and we protect your tax position as carefully as our own.
  • Experiences path — Recognition & Relationship plus your tier's elected experiences, on personal-fund gifts, with written value disclosure. We hope that many founders will choose this and consider the non-deductible portion money well spent.

You may switch to the Full Deduction path at any time by waiving future experiences.

Questions

Good to know

Is my founding contribution tax-deductible?

Lichen Health is a registered 501(c)(3), and contributions are deductible as the law allows. Here is the honest version, because that's how we do everything: if you choose the Full Deduction path, your entire gift is deductible. If you choose the Experiences path, we state the fair market value of your elected experiences in writing before you commit, and your deduction is your gift minus that value — exactly as the IRS requires, with documentation to match. We're glad to walk through the details with you and your advisors before any commitment.

Can I deepen my commitment over time?

Yes — commitments compound. A member who begins at Substrate is welcomed into Root, Hearth, or beyond as contributions accumulate. There is no penalty for starting small.

What if I want to remain anonymous?

Anonymity is honored at every tier, without exception. You receive the same access and invitations; your name appears nowhere unless you ask for it to.

Can a business, family foundation, or DAF contribute?

Yes — and we coordinate documentation with you and your advisors. One important protection: IRS rules do not allow donors to receive personal benefits (like retreats or ceremonies) in connection with gifts from donor-advised funds, private foundations, or IRA distributions. Gifts through these vehicles are honored on the Full Deduction path — complete Recognition & Relationship, full tier standing, no experiences. Many founders pair vehicles: DAF gifts for tier standing, a personal-fund gift when they want to elect experiences. We'll help you structure it cleanly.

What if I don't want any perks at all?

Then you are our favorite kind of complicated. Choose the Full Deduction path at any tier — full recognition, full relationship, full deduction, nothing else. You can also waive recognition itself and give in complete anonymity.

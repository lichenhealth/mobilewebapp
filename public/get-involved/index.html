Lichen grows by invitation

Every member arrives through someone already here — woven into their web from day one. It’s how a network stays trustworthy enough to hand your care, your work and your exchanges to. Know someone inside? Ask them for an invitation. Don’t yet? Introduce yourself at the door — tell us who you are and what draws you here, and a real person will write back.

Every new member receives three months of full Concierge membership, free — the complete platform: your communities and groups, the marketplace, courses and library, events and calendars, concierge care, and a web of people you trust. No card required. When your three months end, choose the membership that fits, right inside the app. And because it's early, your voice carries: founding members tell us which features they need and help us test the platform — you won't just use Lichen, you'll shape it.
